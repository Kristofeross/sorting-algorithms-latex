\chapter*{Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Summary}

The first chapter introduces the subject of the study and defines its main objective and scope.

The second chapter presents the theoretical foundations of parallel sorting, the characteristics of multicore processors 
and~issues related to the performance, efficiency, and~scalability of parallel computing.

The third chapter presents the selected sorting algorithms:
Parallel Quick Sort, Parallel Merge Sort, Parallel Bucket Sort, and Parallel Sample Sort,
describing their operating principles and the implementation of parallel computation.

The fourth chapter is devoted to the research environment and the implementation of the algorithms.
It discusses the hardware and system configuration, the technologies used,
the implementation of sequential and parallel variants, as well as the characteristics of the test data.

The fifth chapter presents the research methodology, including test scenarios, measurement parameters 
and~tools used to measure execution time, processor utilization, and RAM usage.

The sixth chapter presents and analyzes the results of the conducted experiments.
The analysis covers execution time, the impact of the number of processing units, resource utilization, 
scalability, and~the influence of input data characteristics on the performance of the examined algorithms.

The seventh chapter presents the main conclusions drawn from the conducted research, discusses the obtained results 
and~identifies the limitations of the experiments and~potential directions for further research.

The final section summarizes the most important results.
